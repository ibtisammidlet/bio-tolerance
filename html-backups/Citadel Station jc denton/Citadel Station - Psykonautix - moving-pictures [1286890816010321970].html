In 1995-96? It competed with Doom II. Doom II had a better core play experience. Duke had versatility, levels, and personality. No mute space marine here. Duke was all over the place, and that difference is precisely what was great about it. Soon Quake I and II came out ... people seriously interested in competitive multiplayer all played Quake, which was the first actual 3d game (Duke and Doom both used sprites in a 2D space to create the illusion of 3d space) but Duke was the best solo game.
