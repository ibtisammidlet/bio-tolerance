5-MeO-MALT

5-MeO-MALT (5-Methoxy-N-methyl-N-allyltryptamine) is a psychedelic tryptamine similar to 5-MeO-DALT and 5-MeO-DMT. It produces profound psychedelic and entheogenic effects while being reported as more gentle than 5-MeO-DMT.

Mechanism of Action

Acts primarily as a serotonin receptor agonist, similar to other 5-MeO compounds[1] Binds to multiple serotonin receptor subtypes including 5-HT1A and 5-HT2A[2] Functions as a monoamine reuptake inhibitor affecting dopamine and serotonin transporters[3]

Primary Effects

Produces profound mystical and transcendent experiences[6] More gentle and manageable compared to 5-MeO-DMT[6] Longer duration allowing for deeper exploration of the experience[6] Enables real-time introspection and reality examination[6] Less overwhelming intensity than 5-MeO-DMT while maintaining similar depth[6]

Physical Effects

Mild nausea during onset, less than 5-MeO-DMT[6] Lower body load compared to 5-MeO-DMT[6] ️ Minimal visual field distortion[6] Maintains bodily coordination at normal doses[6] ❤️ Potential cardiovascular effects including increased heart rate[3]

Comparison to 5-MeO-DMT

Less abrupt onset than 5-MeO-DMT[6] More manageable headspace allowing better integration[6] ⏱️ Longer duration of action[6] Reduced physical discomfort compared to 5-MeO-DMT[6] Better suited for contemplative exploration[6]

Safety Notes

️ Limited research data available on long-term effects[3] Purity concerns due to common brown coloration of available material[6] Requires careful dosing and administration[6] ⚕️ Should not be combined with MAOIs or other serotonergic drugs[2] No published toxicology studies available[5]
